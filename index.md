# Privacy Policy – Apna Setu

**Last updated:** 2026

Apna Setu ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Apna Setu mobile application. This page informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information.

---

## 📌 Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

* Name, email address, and phone number (during registration)
* User profile details
* Service provider details (shop name, location, contact info)
* Device information and app usage data
* Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token for notifications

---

## 🔧 How We Use Information

We use the collected data to:

* Provide and improve our services
* Enable authentication and user accounts
* Connect customers with service providers
* Send notifications and updates
* Maintain app functionality and performance

## 🔐 Data Security

We use secure technologies and Firebase services to protect your personal data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

---

## 🔄 Third-Party Services

We use third-party services such as:

* Firebase Authentication

* Firebase Storage
* Firebase Cloud Messaging

These services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

## 📡 Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share data only:

* To provide core app functionality

Our app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
